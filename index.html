How fishy is this email?

Paste the sender and the message. You get a score from 0 to 10 and the reasons behind it. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Tip: in Gmail, ⋮ → “Show original” gives you the full source.

This is a learning tool, not a guarantee. If an email asks for money or passwords, go to the company’s site by typing its address yourself.
